Prices for men's down perms and iron perms, and women's design perms, volume straightening, setting perms and repair perms.

Home/Perms & Straightening

From down perms to
volume straightening and straightening + setting

We offer a wide perm menu, from basic and design perms to volume straightening and iron perms. Men's menus include down perms, basic and design perms, volume straightening, iron perms, and root and bangs perms; women's menus include basic, design and special perms, plus straightening, setting perms, volume straightening, straightening + setting and repair perms.

Jia specializes in iron and heat perms, Suyeon in men's down perms, Director Eunju in parted perms and volume straightening, Owner Yeonsu Jo in shadow perms and straightening + setting, and Director Soyeon in hippie perms and straightening. For care alongside your perm, see treatment & scalp care.

Recommended for

  • Men who want a down perm (cut included) to tidy the sides
  • Anyone wanting smoother, calmer hair with volume straightening or straightening
  • Those who want curls with an iron perm, setting perm or hippie perm
  • Anyone who wants just a quick root or bangs perm

FAQ

Perm FAQ

How much is a down perm?
A men's down perm (cut included) is ₩35,000, and a full down perm (cut included) is ₩50,000.
How much is volume straightening?
Men's volume straightening is ₩100,000 for healthy hair and ₩120,000 for damaged hair; women's volume straightening is ₩120,000 for healthy hair and ₩140,000 for damaged hair. Women's straightening + setting perm is ₩140,000 for healthy hair and ₩180,000 for damaged hair.
Are there discounts on perms?
Full color and heat perms are up to 25% off when you use Naver Booking or Naver Pay, or leave a Naver review.
