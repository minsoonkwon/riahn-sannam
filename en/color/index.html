From root color to
bleach and black color removal

We offer premium root and full color (gray coverage and fashion color), basic and design bleach, black color removal, hair manicure and coating, plus separate men's color and bleach menus.

Owner Yeonsu Jo specializes in ash brown and chocolate brown, Director Eunju in brown shades, and Director Soyeon in blue-black color. For care with your color, see treatment & scalp care.

Recommended for

  • Anyone who wants a neat root touch-up
  • Those looking for gray coverage or fashion color
  • Anyone preparing for a bright color with bleach or black color removal
  • Those who want ash brown, chocolate brown or blue-black

Hair Color FAQ

How much are root color and full color?
Premium root color (under 2 cm) is ₩50,000 and premium fashion root color is ₩60,000. Full color is ₩65,000 for premium gray coverage and ₩75,000 for premium fashion color. Men's premium root color is ₩40,000.
How much is bleach?
Basic root bleach (under 2 cm) is ₩50,000, basic bleach ₩90,000 per session, design bleach ₩130,000 per session and black color removal ₩150,000 per session.
Are there discounts on color?
Full color and heat perms are up to 25% off when you use Naver Booking or Naver Pay, or leave a Naver review.
