About

From cuts to treatments,
RIAHN Hair Cheongju Sannam

We offer a wide range of hair services, from cuts, perms and color to treatments. Book, pay or leave a review on Naver and get up to 25% off heat perms and full color.

Our length surcharges are clearly listed, so you can check the cost before your service. With scalp care and a range of hair treatments, you can also look after your hair once your service is done.

Open since 2007 with 6 styling chairs, we are on the 1st floor of 25-1 Tapgol-ro, Seowon-gu, Cheongju-si, Chungcheongbuk-do — on the street in front of the shops at the rear gate of Daewon Cantavil 2nd complex, Sannam-dong.
