FAQ

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about booking, parking, hours and prices.

Home/FAQ
How do I book?
Book your date and time on Naver Booking. You can also contact us by phone (+82-507-1424-0790) or with the inquiry button on Naver Place.
Is parking available?
The salon has parking for up to 2.5 cars, and you can also park nearby.
What are your hours?
We are open 10:00–19:30 on weekdays, Saturdays and Sundays. Please call before your visit to check our regular closing days.
Are some services only available at certain times?
Yes. Haircuts are available 10:00–19:00, color 10:00–18:00, perms 10:00–17:30, and straightening and setting perms 10:00–16:00.
How much do services cost?
Haircuts ₩15,000–35,000 (bangs trim ₩5,000), perms and straightening ₩25,000–200,000, color ₩40,000–150,000, treatments ₩35,000–100,000, and scalp care from ₩60,000 per session (scalp care keeping ₩120,000 when purchased).
Do you have student prices?
Yes. For elementary to high school students, a basic cut is ₩15,000 and a design cut is ₩16,000. Kids' cuts (age 7 and under) are ₩20,000.
Do you offer discounts?
Full color and heat perms are up to 25% off when you use Naver Booking or Naver Pay, or leave a Naver review.
Is there an extra charge for long hair?
Length surcharges are ₩0 for short hair, ₩10,000 for bob length, ₩20,000 for shoulder to chest length and ₩30,000 for hair below the chest.
Can I book a specific designer?
Choose your designer when you book. Each designer's specialties are on the Our Designers page.
Do you offer scalp care?
Yes. Scalp care starts from ₩60,000 per session, and scalp care keeping is ₩120,000 when purchased and ₩40,000 per session.

Didn't find your answer? Call us during opening hours (10:00–19:30).
