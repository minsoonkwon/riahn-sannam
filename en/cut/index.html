Home/Haircuts

From layered cuts to two-block cuts,
haircuts in Sannam-dong

We list women's and men's basic and design cuts, student cuts (elementary to high school), kids' cuts (age 7 and under) and scalp scaling cuts separately. We also offer blow-dry and iron styling, as well as basic and scalp scaling shampoo menus.

Owner Yeonsu Jo specializes in layered and see-through dandy cuts, Director Eunju in bob and two-block cuts, and Director Soyeon in layered cuts. If you want volume too, see perms and volume straightening in Sannam-dong.

Recommended for

  • Anyone wanting a design cut such as a layered cut or bob
  • Men looking for a see-through dandy cut or two-block cut
  • Students (elementary to high school) and kids aged 7 and under
  • Those who want a blow-dry or iron styling with their cut

FAQ

Haircut FAQ

How much is a student haircut?
For elementary to high school students, a basic cut is ₩15,000 for both boys and girls, and a design cut is ₩16,000.
Do you cut children's hair?
Yes. A basic cut for girls aged 7 and under and a cut for boys aged 7 and under are ₩20,000 each.
Do you have designers who specialize in layered or two-block cuts?
Owner Yeonsu Jo specializes in layered and see-through dandy cuts, Director Eunju in bob and two-block cuts, and Director Soyeon in layered cuts.
